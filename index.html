Battery Intelligence

In partnership with Worthless.Dev, our Secure Battery Intelligence Platform represents custom hardware development and vertically integrated data ecosystem tailored for the high-performance, long-range Light Electric Vehicle (LEV) sector. It facilitates the translation of raw cell data into actionable cloud analytics, thereby supporting the evolution toward Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) rental/leasing-to-own strategies and delivering technical assurance for fleet administration and battery leasing initiatives.

Core Technical Innovations

The Data Translation Layer (Multi-Protocol Interoperability)

RAC and Worthless.Dev are currently developing a proprietary hardware and software stack to translate disparate data streams from three common Battery Management Systems (BMS), including JBD, ANT, and JK, into a unified telemetry format. This layer enables seamless monitoring of diverse fleet assets, ensuring that various hardware brands communicate with a central server using a standardized protocol.

Cellular-Edge Telemetry

Battery units will incorporate tamper-proof, dedicated low-power cellular communication modules, facilitating real-time transmission of key operational data, including State of Charge, State of Health, and Thermal Profiles, directly to a central cloud platform. This continuous "Always-On" data stream eliminates the need for manual Bluetooth synchronization and enables enhanced predictive battery safety monitoring and proactive fleet management capabilities.

Predictive Health Monitoring & Smart Alerts

Furthermore, RAC/Worthless.Dev is developing a backend monitoring platform. This platform employs sophisticated filtering techniques to eliminate "Critical" and "Warning" alerts stemming from transient electrical noise. The system is designed to alert operators only to actionable alarms related to actual safety concerns or performance issues, enabling the efficient management of hundreds of leased assets with reduced technical burden.
